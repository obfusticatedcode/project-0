How to Play Checkers

The objective of the game is to block your opponent from making any legal moves or to capture all of their pieces. Here are the rules:

  • The game is played on an 8x8 checkerboard with alternating light and dark squares. Only the dark squares are used.
  • Each player starts with 12 pieces placed on their first three rows of dark squares.
  • Pieces move diagonally forward into an unoccupied square.
  • If an opponent's piece is diagonally adjacent and the square beyond it is empty, the piece can jump over the opponent's piece, capturing it. Multiple jumps are allowed if the conditions are met.
  • When a piece reaches the last row of the opponent's side, it becomes a "King." Kings can move both forward and backward diagonally.
  • The game ends when a player cannot make any legal moves or all their pieces are captured.
  • If neither player can move or capture, the game is a draw.

Good luck and have fun!
